"Why isn't it instant?" Scouts don't call ESPN every second. That would annoy them and slow you down. Instead the latest answer is kept on the page and refreshed on a schedule: hourly while games are on, plus every morning. While somebody's game is on, the Live page checks again every 20 seconds or so (once a minute the rest of the week). Coach's page stamps the time it was "captured", so you can see how fresh it is. Like a sticky note on the fridge: quick to read, updated often. (Nerds call this a cache.)

How it gets onto the internet

COACH

Here's the part everybody skips, and it's my favorite. A website is just a pile of files. Somebody has to put them where the world can see them.

GitHub is the binder where every version of the playbook is saved, with a name, a time, and an undo button. Vercel is the stadium crew: it builds the site and opens the gates. A GitHub robot runs the checks in between.

Is this safe?

COACH

Fair question, and I'm glad you asked. Short version: yes, and here's why.

I can run around all I want, but there's a boundary line on the field. Here's everything standing between a mistake and your league.

Five layers of defense
a mistake, a bad request, a bug
1 · Look, don't touch

I read ESPN and FantasyPros. I can't change a lineup, a roster or anything on ESPN. The only thing I write is my own daily notes, and those go through a pull request.

↓ still here? next layer
2 · Keys in the vault

ESPN needs no key for our public league. The one real secret, the deploy token, lives in GitHub's locked vault. Never in the code, never in my answers.

↓
3 · The rulebook

My playbook spells out what I can do, what I won't, and what to do when I'm not sure. A guard also blocks risky commands outright. When in doubt, I say so.

↓
4 · Test field + safety net

Changes get automated checks and a preview before going live. A failed build never replaces the working site, and there's a rewind button.

↓
5 · A human in charge

Maya approves every recap before it goes up. My daily notes merge on their own after automated checks and a voice check, so if I act up, she tightens the playbook or pulls me off the field.

your league stays fine
